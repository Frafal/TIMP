Principles

One element, one action

Everything that can be tapped does one thing, and it is clear which. A closed stop opens; its circle marks it done. (R1)

Little on screen

Actions grow only by opening, in zones by meaning, never mixed: Go (out of the app) and Change. (R2)

The moment decides

One main action per screen: before and after the trip, adding; on the day itself, going to the next stop. (R3, V2)

Read at a glance

Distinct stops; the time, the name, the place, in the order they are looked for. Made for the street, tired, in the sun. (R13, V5)

Full, dots, dashes

What is there and ahead; where you have been; what is not there yet. Nowhere for decoration. (R15)

For everyone

Every control named, every state said, 44-point targets, contrast checked by a test, in both themes. (A1–A10)

Brand

The logo is the app's icon: a stitched pocket with the trip's things in it, what the trip keeps (its history) and what is travelled together (sharing); "timp" in Inter Bold, the pin over its i. Full bleed: the phone rounds it.

The line of the day

Every city is a line, every stop a station, as on a metro map. The line takes the city's colour; a station shows the stop's number in the colour of its kind of place, the same sign as on the map. Behind you the line turns to dots; ahead it stays full; now is the accent's dot.

The day

The top bar: the trip as the title (a tap opens the list of trips), search, you; under it the days, today called so, and at their end List | Map, in its place on every page of a trip so the bar never changes its size. The heading: the day, its title, its city written as the name of a line, and from today to 3 days ahead its weather (WX1–WX7). The day's line: what matters today in one place, the first and "+N".

Time

A clock time, or one of seven parts of the day, each with its sign: the sun on its arc over the horizon, then the moon. Sunrise and sunset carry their real time where the day is. A stop with no time is all day, at the top of the day (T1–T5).

    On the day

    When the day shown is today, the list starts from now: it scrolls to the next stop when the app opens and when it comes back, never while you scroll; a mark of now says how long until the next stop, which is open; the stops done are in one line. When the next stop's card leaves the screen, its bar comes up at the bottom, within reach of a thumb, with Directions from where you are. Adding is the last row, every day.

    Directions

    The map

    A view of the day, not a panel: List | Map in the top bar. The map takes the heading's place, right under the bar, and stays there while the list scrolls under it, with an edge and a shadow. A pin is a station, the same sign as in the list; a tap brings its stop just under the map. The lines are always there, dots behind you. Two controls: my position, larger or smaller. Larger or smaller, List or Map, push the list: the stop read first stays at its line. Always light. Offline, the sketch.

    Signals

    Four levels; the level gives the colour, the kind the icon. One per closed stop, the first by importance (closed, booking needed, weather, choice, booking advised, label, booked) and "+N"; all of them, each with its action, in the opened stop.

    Search

    In the bar: the lens becomes ✕, the field takes the title's place, only the circle of you stays. The results fill a panel fixed under the bar, which scrolls on its own and does not move as you type. Without accents or case, every word found; a result is the stop's sign, its day and time, its name, the word marked; a tap goes there, open and in view. ✕, Escape or the circle of you close it.

    Sheets

    One sheet for everything done apart: title and × on top, actions at the bottom; a tap outside, × or Escape closes it. It is there on the tap, in its final shape; what comes from far arrives inside it, and such a sheet is tall from the start. Finding positions: the tick takes the point, a tap on the row shows it on a small map with the day's other stops.

    Positions found

    Tap a row to see it on the map, with the day's other stops. Untick the wrong ones.

    Trips

    A trip's card opens the trip; ⋯ opens its sheet, with everything else. Its state in signals, only when there is something to know. Adding: one main action, Paste beside it, the assistant one row.

    Accessibility

    Part of every component, checked where it can be: test/design.test.mjs holds the contrast of every pair in both themes; tools/e2e.mjs checks in the browser that every control has a name, every state is said, every target is 44 points, headings are in order and no id is used twice.

    • Semantic HTML first: buttons are buttons, links go somewhere, lists are lists.
    • States are said, not only shown: aria-pressed, aria-expanded with aria-controls, aria-checked, aria-current.
    • Focus is visible and follows the order on screen; a sheet keeps it and gives it back.
    • Colour is never alone: a level has its icon and its word.
    • Text grows: sizes in rem; at twice the size rows wrap, they do not overlap.
